JLPT N5

〜てもいいですか

"Is it okay if...?" / "May I...?"

Connection Rule

Verb (て-form) add もいいですか

Explanation

This grammar structure is used to ask for permission to do something. It implies a polite request, checking if an action is acceptable to the listener. To form it, conjugate the verb into its 'て-form' and attach 'もいいですか'.

Examples

写真を撮ってもいいですか。

Shashin o tottemo ii desu ka.

May I take a picture?

ここに座ってもいいですか。

Koko ni suwattemo ii desu ka.

Is it okay if I sit here?
